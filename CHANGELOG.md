# Spyro Editor 2.3

## Changes since Spyro Editor 2.2

New

- A **Colours** tab beside Game Settings holds everything about looks and moves: this level's colours, Spyro's colours, the egg thieves' colour, Hover, Fly Anywhere and Play as Crash. Memory, names, music and portals stay in Game Settings.
- **This level's colours** (it was Creatures' colours) now lists the open level's **chests**, **dragons** and **animals** as well as its enemies, each list with its own Randomize, and Randomize All for everything. A colour is for that level only, so chests can be blue in one level and red in the next. For dragons, one crystal colour goes on every dragon statue of the level (it used to be set statue by statue in the object's Edit window), and the fairy who waits at a freed dragon's pedestal can be given a colour too.
- **Randomize Every Level** on the same card gives the enemies, chests and animals of every level of the game other colours in one press, each level its own (263 kinds in 35 levels); **Every Level Its Own** puts them all back.
- Enemies and things with no colour of their own (grey metal, stone) can now be given one; they used to be missing from the list.
- **Egg thieves anywhere**: an egg thief added to a level that has none (Add Object › Enemies › Egg Thieves, then New Loop Here for his run) runs, can be caught and gives his egg, in the game's levels and homeworlds.
- **Every egg thief's run can be changed**, the game's own thieves too (select him, then Edit Run Path or drag the numbered points). A point goes onto the ground he runs on and is taken only where he can still run to it and on from it; otherwise it stays put and the editor says why. Water, lava and ooze count as no ground: he could run there, but Spyro sinks and can't catch him. The run card warns when a thief stands in water.
- **The egg count follows the thieves**: every egg thief you add is one more egg in the inventory, in the 100% count and in what Gnasty's Loot's door asks for, and a thief you take out is one fewer. Create BIN says how many eggs the game has when it is not the game's 12. A level without its own thief can still be finished 100%.
- **Spring chests that hold a teal or a rainbow gem**: Add Object › Interactive › Chests & Containers has **Spring chest (Teal reward)** and **Spring chest (Rainbow reward)**. Flame or charge one and the gem springs up out of the lid; grab it quickly or it vanishes and the chest closes again, as the game's spring chests do. It adds 50 or 100 to the treasure and to the level's total, in the game's own levels and in levels you build.
- **Random levels and homeworlds are far more varied**: each seed picks one of seven layouts (a way from room to room with an end, a ring that comes back over its start, a clearing with rooms off it, one great room with a hill, mound or moat, a hill of terraces to climb, a winding gorge, a web of rooms with more than one way round), rooms from about 300 to 1,150 units across, and new set pieces: flights of steps, long climbing paths, stepped mounds, raised terraces, hollows, islets and moats, castle yards. Levels rise and fall about half as much again as before. **Laid out as** in the Random Level window chooses the layout yourself. Homeworlds use the same layouts. The same seed makes a different level than in 2.2.
- **Random objects places things more like the game**: chests and gems up on raised places and perches, keys on perches, the extra life and locked chest tucked away, enemies spread through the rooms with treasure mostly out of their reach, gems in little knots as well as lines, and nothing on banks or in the sea.
- **Teal and rainbow gems in Random objects**: tick **Teal gems (50)** or **Rainbow gems (100)** and they go alone to the hardest places to reach, counted in the treasure you asked for.
- **Trees, torches, flowers** in Random objects (on unless unticked): the level type's own trees, torches, lamps, flowers and grass, placed where the game puts them.
- **Memory is Emulator (8 MB RAM) unless you choose otherwise**, so levels hold more and fewer changes run out of room. A game made this way still plays on a normal 2 MB set-up wherever its levels fit 2 MB, and Create BIN says when one does not. Choose **PlayStation (2 MB)** in Game Settings › Memory for a real console or a disc. A project that never chose now gets 8 MB.
- Create BIN places the way into your new world by itself when there is none yet (on open ground near where Spyro arrives in the homeworld; move it with Terrain › World Entrance). It used to stop there.
- Fix It For Me leaves out far less. When a level's objects stop Create BIN, it now finds which **kinds of object you added** are the cause (every copy of one creature, say) and leaves out only those; your other objects and your changes to the level's own objects stay in the game. It tries the added kinds in halves, which takes a few more runs of Create BIN.
- When Fix It For Me has left anything out, a window opens that says what and why, with Put Back beside each thing. It is no longer only a line at the bottom of the window.
- After Create BIN the status says how much room the levels you added objects to have left, the fullest first, and warns when one is nearly full.

Fixed

- Moving a point of one of the game's own egg thieves' runs made Create BIN stop ("egg-thief path export blocked … clearance"), even for a small drag: the check compared heights the game never uses, and edits to the game's own runs were refused anyway. Such runs are now judged the way the game runs them.

- A spring chest added with Add Object (a plain one too) made Create BIN stop in Stone Hill with "Object build blocked by the native Moby safety check" when the project had no level you built: it was wrongly given the safety profile of an older way of importing chests. Chests from Add Object are checked by their own fit check now, and a project saved with such a chest is made whole as it is.
- A doorway with a shape (Terrain › Doorways, or a generated homeworld) sometimes did not load its level: Spyro was lifted into it and then flew on through and fell. The game flies Spyro in along its original doorway's path at the original's height, and Stone Hill's from Artisans runs high, along the top of a smaller shape's opening. Spyro now flies in through the lower part of every shaped opening (checked for every shape at every size on every homeworld's doorways), and Create BIN builds the way into your new world the same way.
- A homeworld with the way into your new world and an added object that brings its own sounds or sparks (a Crash frog or crate, a creature from another level) made Create BIN stop with "New World doorway failed its readback: particles and sounds changed". Fix It For Me then got the game made by leaving out every object change of that level. The check was wrong, not the level: such levels are made whole now.
- The export check said "Ready to export" when all it had checked was your object changes. It now says what it checked, and that Create BIN checks memory, doorways and your world while it builds.
